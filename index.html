Six months of AI-native delivery

I adopt new tooling fast, and AI is the clearest case. In roughly six months I delivered platform scope that normally runs multi-year: Cursor cloud agents driving the automation, LangGraph orchestrating the complex multi-step loops, and custom agent plugins and skills I built for my team to use directly. Here is the method, in the order it actually works.

Runbooks turned into agent skills — cluster creation, bootstrap, and app onboarding chained into a single run.

LangGraph

Custom plugins & skills
